🗑️ Delete Your Account & Data

App: Homestead: Idle Farm Tycoon (com.homestead.farm) · Developer: Casayoung Development

Homestead works fully without an account. An account only exists if you chose to sign in with Google to use cloud save and the friends ranking. This page explains how to delete that data.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app (immediate)

  • Open Homestead.
  • Tap the profile button in the top bar (top right of the screen — it shows your name once you are signed in).
  • Tap 🗑️ Delete cloud data and confirm.

This immediately erases, from our servers:

  • your cloud save (the backup of your farm),
  • your public ranking profile (farm name, era, lifetime Labour, ribbons),
  • your farm-name reservation.

Your farm stays on your device unless you also clear it (below). You can sign out instead if you only want to stop syncing.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

Email casayoung.dev@gmail.com from (or naming) the Google account you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Homestead data”. We will delete your cloud save, ranking profile and name reservation, and confirm by reply, within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is not

  • Deleted: cloud save, public ranking profile, farm-name reservation, and the Firebase account link we hold for you.
  • Not deleted: your local save on your device — remove it with Android Settings → Apps → Homestead → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Not affected: your Google Account itself. Deleting Homestead data does not delete or alter your Google Account.
  • Advertising data: handled by Google, not stored by us — reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What we keep, and for how long

Once deleted, the data above is removed from our Cloud Firestore database. We keep no separate copy. Routine encrypted backups held by our hosting provider (Google) may persist for a short period before being overwritten in the normal course of operations, and aggregate, non-identifying statistics (such as total installs) are unaffected.
